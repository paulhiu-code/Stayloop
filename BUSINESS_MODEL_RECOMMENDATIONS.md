# StayLoop Business Model Recommendations

## Positioning

StayLoop should lead with the guest marketplace: search, verified stays, transparent pricing, secure payments, mobile trip management, and reliable support. The host revenue-share program should live on a separate partner page so guests do not feel like they are booking through a recruiting business.

## Recommended marketplace fees

- Host service fee: 10% of booking subtotal.
- Guest service fee: 5% shown before checkout.
- Host payout timing: collect guest payment upfront and release the host payout about 24 hours after check-in, assuming no fraud, cancellation, or support hold.
- Payment processor: Stripe Connect is the best starting point for marketplace payments, delayed payouts, platform fees, identity/KYC, tax reporting, refunds, and disputes.

## Recommended referral split

Use a 3-2-1 structure from the 10% host service fee:

| Level | Referral relationship | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Direct referred host | 3% |
| Level 2 | Host referred by a Level 1 host | 2% |
| Level 3 | Host referred by a Level 2 host | 1% |

Reasons:

- It rewards direct acquisition most.
- It keeps deeper network incentives meaningful without overpaying passive levels.
- It leaves 4 percentage points for StayLoop operations, support, insurance, payment costs, compliance, and product development.
- It is easy for hosts to understand and easier to audit than many-tier programs.

## Suggested referral rules

- Qualified referrer: verified host with at least one active StayLoop property.
- Attribution: referral link or invite code, last qualified touch before host signup, with admin override for sales-led deals.
- Lock-in: one referrer per host account or tax entity after verification.
- Payout status: pending at booking, eligible after the underlying host payout clears.
- Anti-gaming: block self-referrals, circular referrals, duplicate tax entities, shell accounts, related-party abuse, fake stays, and refunded or cancelled bookings.

## Compliance and risk posture

- Start as a marketplace, but add compliance tooling for registration reminders, occupancy tax notices, local permit fields, and documentation storage.
- Replicate Airbnb-style protection positioning through host verification, guest screening, secure payments, damage protection, claims workflows, and support escalation.
- Require hosts to represent that they have the right to list the property and maintain required insurance.
